Welcome to Finance Tracker



Many people struggle to monitor their spending until they exceed their budget, often due to a lack of awareness of their financial habits. Without real-time tracking, budget planning, or alerts, it’s hard to see where money is going, leading to poor financial decisions. This Finance Tracker web application will allow users to record their income and expenses and analyze spending trends. It will also provide budget notifications, helping users make informed financial choices, avoid impulse spending, and gain better control over their finances.
